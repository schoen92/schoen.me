Start with jobs, pains, and outcomes.

Then add two operational layers:

- Funnel stage (discover, evaluate, adopt, expand)
- Motion owner (product, support, marketing, sales)

Keep labels few and useful. Taxonomies fail when they optimize for completeness instead of decisions.
