title: "A Data Quality Checklist for Customer Research Pipelines"
excerpt: "Prevent bad inputs from creating confident but wrong decisions."
date: "2026-04-10"
tags: ["data-quality", "analytics"]
---

Trust comes from reliable inputs.

Before analysis, check:

- Source duplication
- Language drift
- Timestamp validity
- Missing context fields
- Label consistency

If one of these breaks, narrative quality drops fast.
