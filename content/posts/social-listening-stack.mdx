Most teams collect more mentions than they can use.

A useful stack has three layers:

- Collection: pull from a focused set of communities and channels.
- Structuring: normalize text, source, theme, sentiment, and urgency.
- Activation: route patterns to product, support, and growth owners.

The win is not volume. The win is speed to confident action.
